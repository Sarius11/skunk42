## Brief

A three-dimensional Earth, built on CesiumJS and dressed in NASA's Blue Marble imagery, one cloud-free mosaic for each month. On top of it goes the weather: real clouds from the five geostationary satellites that watch the planet, stitched onto one grid.

Six-hourly model data is not live enough. The satellites go first; atmospheric models only fill the gaps the satellites can't see.

<p class="dim">An earlier version stalled on exactly two things: the data feeds and believable clouds. This version starts with both.</p>
